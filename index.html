Download on App Store Contact Support

Features

Everything you need, all in one canvas

∞

Infinite Canvas

Pan, zoom, and organize freely without page limits. Fixed sizes like A4, Letter, and iPad are also available.

✏️

Drawing & Sketching

Draw freely anywhere on the canvas with Apple Pencil or your finger. Card-style drawing boards also supported.

📝

Text & Sticky Notes

Add rich text with custom fonts and colors. Sticky notes with list styles keep quick thoughts organized.

✅

Todo Lists

Create task lists with subtasks, priorities, and due dates directly on your canvas.

📄

PDF & Images

Import PDFs and images directly onto your canvas. View PDFs in a full reader without leaving the app.

🎵

Audio Recording

Record voice notes or import audio files and place them anywhere on your canvas.

🔗

Connectors

Draw connections between any elements to build mind maps, flowcharts, and concept diagrams.

☁️

Cloud Sync

Sign in with Apple to sync all your canvases across iPhone, iPad, and Mac automatically.

📤

Export to PNG

Export any canvas as a high-resolution PNG image to share or save.

Works across all your Apple devices
